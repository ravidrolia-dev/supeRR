## 1. Executive Summary: Strategic Context for IPL 2027

To transition from playoff contenders to sustained IPL champions, Rajasthan Royals' recruitment team requires an objective diagnostic of their structural roster dependencies. 

While the franchise possesses world-class individual talents at the top of the order (Sanju Samson, Yashasvi Jaiswal, Riyan Parag), multi-season ball-by-ball analysis reveals **three systemic tactical vulnerabilities under acute match pressure**:
1. **Death Overs Finishing Void (Overs 17–20):** Over-reliance on Shimron Hetmyer creates a single point of failure when chasing rates exceed $11.5\text{ rpo}$.
2. **Death Overs Bowling Execution (Overs 17–20):** Beyond Sandeep Sharma's medium-pace variations, the squad lacks an express death bowler capable of executing yorkers at $>145\text{ km/h}$ under extreme leverage ($\text{BPI} \ge 65.0$).
3. **Powerplay Finger-Spin Run Choking:** With historical dependence on classical wrist-spin, RR often conceded high early Powerplay boundaries against left-handed openers without a power-hitting finger spinner to exploit match-ups.

## 3. The Three Critical Squad Gaps Defined

### Gap 1: The Elite Death-Overs Finisher (Batting Order Position: #6 or #7)
- **Role Requirement:** High boundary percentage against $140+\text{ km/h}$ pace and reverse-swing yorkers in overs 17–20.
- **ClutchIQ Performance Threshold:** Career High-Pressure Strike Rate $>175.0$; V3 Pressure Performance contribution $>20.0$.
- **Target Profile:** **Tim David** (#4 Batter, Strong Evidence, HP SR: 184.2) or **Naman Dhir** (#5 Batter, Strong Evidence, Indian utility).

### Gap 2: The Express Death-Overs Strike Enforcer (Bowling Phase: Overs 16, 18, 20)
- **Role Requirement:** Uncompromising pace, high dot-ball percentage, and proven wicket-taking ability at $\text{BPI} \ge 65.0$.
- **ClutchIQ Performance Threshold:** High-pressure wicket rate $<14$ balls/wicket; Defensive Runs Saved $>+30.0$.
- **Target Profile:** **Matheesha Pathirana** (#4 Bowler, Moderate Evidence, Sling Yorker Specialist) or **Mark Wood** (#2 Bowler, 27 HP Legal Balls, $+32.86$ Shrunk Rating).

### Gap 3: The Powerplay Run-Strangle Spin All-Rounder (Phase: Overs 1–8)
- **Role Requirement:** Early overs economy $<6.5\text{ rpo}$, high dot-ball frequency, and lower-order batting flexibility.
- **ClutchIQ Performance Threshold:** Bowler PPI $>+10.0$; Defensive Par Surplus creation.
- **Target Profile:** **Sunil Narine** (#7 Bowler, Moderate Evidence) or **Imad Wasim** (#3 Bowler, $+24.83$ PPI).

## 4. Squad Redundancies & Non-Priorities
- **Top-Order Indian LHB Accumulators:** With Yashasvi Jaiswal established and Vaibhav Sooryavanshi under franchise development, RR should **not** allocate primary auction resources toward top-order openers (e.g. Priyansh Arya or Abhishek Sharma), despite their high pressure scores.
- **Opening Swing Bowlers:** Trent Boult and Sandeep Sharma already provide elite Powerplay swing containment. Resources must be diverted to the death phase.
