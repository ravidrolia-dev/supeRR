ClutchIQ strictly maintains two independent evaluation engines:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

│    70% V3 Pressure Performance + 20% PPI + 10% Consistency  │

                              ≠
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 2. RR SQUAD FIT ASSESSMENT (Needs-Based)                    │
│    Role alignment, phase demand, tactical gap resolution,   │
│    and Indian / Overseas roster balance                      │

```

A player may achieve a #1 Integrated Pressure ranking globally but hold a lower auction priority for Rajasthan Royals if their batting phase overlaps with established assets (e.g. Sanju Samson or Yashasvi Jaiswal). Conversely, a player with moderate direct pressure evidence may address an urgent structural crisis (e.g. death overs pace enforcement). Both vectors are displayed transparently in player dossiers.
