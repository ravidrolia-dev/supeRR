## 1. Executive Summary: What Actually Happened to RR in IPL 2026?

A rigorous ball-by-ball analysis of all 16 matches contested by Rajasthan Royals in the 2026 Indian Premier League reveals a team of **stark polarities**:
- **The World-Class Engine:** Rajasthan Royals fielded the most devastating Powerplay batting combination in IPL history. Emerging prodigy **Vaibhav Sooryavanshi (776 runs, SR 238.04)** paired with **Yashasvi Jaiswal (427 runs, SR 152.50)** propelled RR to a league-best Powerplay scoring rate of **187.33 (9.37 rpo)**.
- **The Fatal Vulnerability:** RR suffered from an **acute bowling collapse in the death overs (overs 17–20)** and an inability to defend totals. In 5 of their 7 losses, RR batted first, set competitive totals (averaging 185+), and failed to defend due to death-bowling run leakage.
- **The Sandeep Sharma & Deshpande Regression:** While Jofra Archer remained world-class (25 wickets, economy 9.12), historical death specialist Sandeep Sharma conceded **13.70 rpo in death overs** across 6 appearances, while Tushar Deshpande leaked **12.38 rpo**. This forced RR to rely excessively on rookie Brijesh Sharma (economy 10.52 in death).
- **Finishing & Lower-Order Instability:** Shimron Hetmyer experienced a severe form slump (78 runs in 7 matches, SR 113.04), forcing Donovan Ferreira (317 runs, SR 179.10) and Ravindra Jadeja (266 runs, SR 135.71) to absorb high-pressure chases without established boundary hitters at #7.
