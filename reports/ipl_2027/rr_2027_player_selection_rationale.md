# RR 2027 Auction Player Selection Rationale

## 1. Executive Summary & Selection Philosophy

This document outlines the evidence-based selection rationale for Rajasthan Royals' IPL 2027 auction targets.

In strict compliance with **Task 11.3**, players are **not selected merely because of a high ClutchIQ integrated ranking**. Every candidate in the final shortlist must satisfy five rigorous criteria:
1. **Directly addresses a verified 2026 Rajasthan Royals campaign weakness** (W1 Death Bowling Concession, W2 Finishing Collapse, W3 Finger-Spin Run Choking, W4 Heavy Overseas Pace Reliance).
2. **Possesses verified high-pressure performance evidence** under ClutchIQ's 70/20/10 scoring methodology ($BPI \ge 65$).
3. **Offers clear tactical deployment compatibility** without creating role redundancy.
4. **Maintains realistic availability awareness** with explicit franchise association mapping.
5. **Enforces disciplined auction passes** on high-profile openers to prevent purse misallocation.
