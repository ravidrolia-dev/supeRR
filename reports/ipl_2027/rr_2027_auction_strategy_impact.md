## 1. Tactical Alignment with Verified Squad Reality

Reconstructing the definitive 2026 Rajasthan Royals roster produces three major strategic conclusions that refine the IPL 2027 auction roadmap:

### 1.1 The Sanju Samson Trade Reality: Parag as Franchise Captain
- **Finding:** Sanju Samson was already at Chennai Super Kings throughout 2026, while Ravindra Jadeja anchored RR's lower-middle order and Riyan Parag took the captaincy armband in 14 matches.
- **Auction Impact:** ClutchIQ does not need to design a transition plan away from Samson; that transition occurred prior to 2026. Parag is established as captain and Jaiswal as vice-captain. The auction focus remains strictly on plugging operational death-overs weaknesses.

### 1.2 The Sam Curran Void: Right-Handed Pace Power-Finisher
- **Finding:** RR bought Sam Curran for ₹2.40 Cr to bat at #6/#7 and bowl death overs, but his pre-season groin injury forced RR into signing Dasun Shanaka as an emergency backup.
- **Auction Impact:** RR's finishing weakness in 2026 was not an inherent failure of squad strategy, but the direct fallout of losing Curran and Hetmyer's concurrent form collapse.
- **2027 Solution:** Replacing this void requires an elite high-pressure finisher. The performance-first ranking identifies **Tim David** (V3 Score: 70.36, 497 HP balls, SR 184.2) or domestic prodigy **Naman Dhir** (Score: 66.85, 220 HP balls) as the ideal tactical solutions for Priority R2.

### 1.3 The True Pace Depth: Why Overseas Quota Must Shift to the Death
- **Finding:** RR retained Jofra Archer, Nandre Burger, and Kwena Maphaka while buying Adam Milne. However, only Archer possessed true death-over containment quality. With Sandeep Sharma (Econ 13.70 in death) and Deshpande (12.38) failing, RR suffered 5 fatal 2nd-innings collapses.
- **Auction Impact:** RR does not need more new-ball swing bowlers. RR specifically requires a specialist who executes sub-9.00 rpo in overs 18 & 20.
- **2027 Solution:** **Matheesha Pathirana** (V3 Bowler Score: 73.19, 82 HP balls, toe-crushing yorker specialist) or **Mark Wood** (Score: 70.44, 27 HP balls, express >150 km/h).
