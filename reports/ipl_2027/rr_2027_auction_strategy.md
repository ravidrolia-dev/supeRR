  Shimron Hetmyer suffered a severe decline in 2026, scoring only **78 runs in 7 innings at a strike rate of 113.04**. RR lost 34 wickets in death overs at just 10.9 balls per dismissal.
- **Recommended Solution: Tim David (Score: 70.67 | Strong Evidence, 497 HP Balls):**
