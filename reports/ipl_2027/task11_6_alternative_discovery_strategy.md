### Requirement 1: Express Death Yorker Bowler (Priority: CRITICAL)
*Addresses W1 (Death Concession: Sandeep 13.70 rpo, Deshpande 12.38 rpo).*

- **Primary Target (Overseas):** **Matheesha Pathirana** (Score: 66.37, 82 HP balls | Tier: Moderate Direct)

  - *Profile:* Elite sling-action yorker specialist with 9.15 death economy and exceptional dot-ball suppression.
- **Second Alternative (Overseas):** **Nathan Ellis** (Score: 62.66, 84 HP balls | Tier: Moderate Direct)
  - *Franchise:* Chennai Super Kings (Pending Nov 17 Retention).
  - *Profile:* Back-of-the-hand slower balls and yorkers; robust death pressure track record in high-leverage situations.
- **Indian Domestic Alternative:** **Mohsin Khan** (Score: 62.34, 35 HP balls | Tier: Moderate Direct)
  - *Franchise:* Lucknow Super Giants (Pending Nov 17 Retention).
  - *Profile:* Tall left-arm fast bowler generating steep bounce and natural angle into right-handers; delivers high dot-ball pressure.
- **Uncontracted Watchlist:** **Mark Wood** (Score: 73.89, 27 HP balls) & **Joshua Little** (Score: 61.81, 30 HP balls).
- **Tactical Trade-off:**
  Opting for Mohsin Khan preserves an overseas spot for a finisher like Tim David or Glenn Maxwell, but carries higher injury history risk compared to Nathan Ellis.
