## 3. Detailed Scouting Dossiers: Priority Targets for Rajasthan Royals

### 3.1 Tim David (Australia | RHB Finisher)
- **Integrated Pressure Score:** **70.67** (Rank #4)
- **High-Pressure Evidence:** **497 High-Pressure Legal Balls Faced** (Strong Direct Evidence, $95\%\text{ CI}: [23.1, 40.1]$).
- **RR Squad Requirement Addressed:** **Critical Gap 1 (Death-Overs Power Finishing).**
- **Tactical Contribution:**  
  Tim David is the prototype modern boundary finisher. In death overs (overs 17–20), his strike rate exceeds $190.0$ against express pace. Positioning David at #6 alongside Shimron Hetmyer gives Rajasthan Royals a devastating twin-finisher axis that forces opposition captains to defend both sides of the ground.
- **Risks & Mitigation:** Vulnerable to leg-spin away from his body early in his innings. RR must utilize Samson and Parag to absorb spin in overs 11–15, sheltering David for pure pace execution.

### 3.2 Matheesha Pathirana (Sri Lanka | Right-Arm Sling Fast)
- **Integrated Pressure Score:** **66.37** (Rank #4 Bowler)
- **High-Pressure Evidence:** **82 High-Pressure Legal Deliveries Bowled** (Moderate Direct Evidence, $95\%\text{ CI}: [12.3, 30.3]$).
- **RR Squad Requirement Addressed:** **Critical Gap 2 (Express Death-Overs Wicket Taker).**
- **Tactical Contribution:**  
  Pathirana's low sling release creates late dip and reverse swing that is virtually impossible to elevate in overs 18 and 20. Pairing Pathirana's express yorkers ($>145\text{ km/h}$) with Sandeep Sharma's dipping knuckle-balls gives RR the most complete, varied death-bowling pair in the league.
- **Risks & Mitigation:** Prone to wide delivery extras when attempting wide yorkers under pressure. Requires clear fielding captaincy to establish set field angles.

Furthermore, his off-spin provides a viable match-up weapon against opposition left-handers in overs 7–10.

### 3.4 Mark Wood (England | Right-Arm Express Fast)
- **Integrated Pressure Score:** **73.89** (Rank #2 Bowler)
- **High-Pressure Evidence:** **27 High-Pressure Legal Deliveries** (Moderate Direct Evidence, Shrunk Rating: **+32.86**).

- **Risks & Mitigation:** High injury vulnerability. RR must pair him with a durable domestic backup and limit his regular season workload to 3 overs per match.
